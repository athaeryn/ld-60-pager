#### STAGE 01

You are Susan, a city girl who—with your wife Ellen—bought a farm out of foreclosure.

> [!NOTE] General Gameplay Notes
>
> - When you read the pager, you stand still.
> - The time on the pager will start at 1:14 and then a timer will increment it every minute, so it advances in real time.

You wake up in your bed to the BEEP BEEP BEEP of a PAGER going off.
You turn and see it on the nightstand.
It's DARK the middle of the night.
Sitting up, you reach for it…

> Um.. I don't remember having a pager.

> [!NOTE] Gameplay Note
> Introduce the "Raise Pager" mechanic: play an animation of the pager coming toward the camera, but ending up in the mostly-off-screen position, still beeping, and show a text hint like "press \_\_\_ to look at pager".

You pick up the pager and look at the screen:

```

    Rise and shine, Susan.

01:14 AM              10/18/26
```

> What the hell?

You get up from the bed.
Turn around and see it empty.

> [!NOTE] Gameplay Note
> The player starts fully controlling the character's movement right after this programmed turn toward the bed.

> Where's Ellen? Something's wrong.

#### STAGE 02

> [!attention] Trigger
> LEAVING the BEDROOM (or maybe just the bed area) triggers the next message.

```
Thrilling to see you're up.
You won't have all day.

0X:XX AM              10/18/26
```

> [!NOTE] Gameplay Note
> At this point the player can discover the "Eat Crow" mechanic—whereby the Scarecrow will ambush and stab you if you ignore or disobey his messages for too long, try to run away, etc.—at any time if they don't respond to the pager, etc.
